Security

Two-step login

With this on, logging in needs your password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Authy, or the iPhone Passwords app). A stolen password alone won't get anyone in.

Logged-in devices

Every device stays logged in for 30 days. If you lost a phone or shared a computer, log the others out.

Also do this

Use a long password you don't use anywhere else (16+ characters). Change it in Railway → your HQ service → Variables → APP_PASSWORD. Turn on two-step login for Railway and Upstash too, since they hold your data.
